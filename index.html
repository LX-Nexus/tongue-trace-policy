1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) explains how Lexxis (Pty) Ltd (“Lexxis”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects personal information gathered through Tongue Trace, a conversational data-collection platform (“the Platform” or “Tongue Trace”).

Tongue Trace is being developed as part of a collaboration between Revelation Spiritual Home, Lexxis, the GSMA, and [Name of Third-Party Partner] (together, the “Collaborating Parties”), for the purpose of building a dataset of spoken and written African languages and using that dataset to train artificial intelligence (“AI”) language models.

This Policy applies to any individual (“Participant”, “you”, or “your”) who interacts with Tongue Trace, whether through WhatsApp or any other messaging channel through which the Platform may operate.

2. Scope and Application

Tongue Trace is currently used to collect voice and text data in isiZulu and Xhosa from Participants based in South Africa. The Platform is intended to expand over time to additional languages and to other countries in which Revelation Spiritual Home operates.

Where Tongue Trace is used in a country other than South Africa, this Policy will be reviewed and, where necessary, supplemented or amended to comply with the data protection laws applicable in that country. Participants in those countries will be given notice of any material changes before further information is collected from them.

This Policy is drafted with primary reference to South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (“POPIA”).

3. Information We Collect

When you interact with Tongue Trace, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Voice recordings: audio of you speaking a phrase or sentence that the Platform prompts you to say, in isiZulu, Xhosa, or another supported language.
  • Text responses: the written translation or transcription you type in response to the same prompt.
  • Contact identifier: the WhatsApp number (or equivalent messaging identifier) used to communicate with you during the conversation. As described in Sections 8 and 9, this is converted into a hashed value before storage and is not retained in plain, readable form.
  • Interaction metadata: such as the date and time of your submissions and the language you were prompted in.

We do not intentionally collect your full name, home address, precise location, national identification number, or other directly identifying information, and we ask that you avoid volunteering such details in your spoken or written responses. Tongue Trace does not request or access your device or WhatsApp location.

4. How We Collect Your Information

Tongue Trace collects information directly from you through a conversational chatbot, currently operating over WhatsApp. The chatbot presents you with a phrase or prompt and asks you to (a) type a translation of that phrase in text, and (b) record yourself saying the phrase, or its translation, aloud. You provide this information voluntarily, and only after you have agreed to this Policy as described in Section 13 (Consent and Withdrawal of Consent).

5. Purpose of Collection and Use

We collect and use your voice recordings and text responses for the following purposes:

  • To build a structured dataset of spoken and written isiZulu, Xhosa, and, as the Platform expands, other African languages.
  • To train, fine-tune, test, and improve AI and machine-learning models, including a model being developed specifically for use by Revelation Spiritual Home.
  • The resulting dataset is intended to be owned by, and/or licensed among, the Collaborating Parties — currently Revelation Spiritual Home, Lexxis, the GSMA, and [Name of Third-Party Partner] — for their own research, product development, and commercial purposes.
  • The dataset, or AI models trained on it, may be licensed, sold, or otherwise commercially exploited by one or more of the Collaborating Parties. This includes the possibility that the dataset generates revenue for the Collaborating Parties.

We will not use your voice recordings or text responses for any purpose other than those described in this Section without seeking your further consent, except where required or permitted by law.

6. Legal Basis for Processing

Our processing of your personal information is based on your voluntary, informed, and specific consent, given before you begin interacting with Tongue Trace (see Section 13). You may withdraw this consent at any time.

7. Who We Share Your Information With

We share information collected through Tongue Trace with:

  • Employees and contractors of Lexxis who are directly involved in building, training, and maintaining the AI models described in this Policy.
  • Third-party data labelling partners engaged by Lexxis to review, annotate, and label voice and text data as part of preparing the dataset for model training.
  • Revelation Spiritual Home and the GSMA, as Collaborating Parties with an ownership or licensing interest in the resulting dataset.

We require that any party we share your information with is bound by data protection obligations that are, at minimum, consistent with this Policy.

8. Anonymisation and De-Identification

Where Tongue Trace operates over WhatsApp, your WhatsApp number is used to facilitate the conversation. Before your information is stored on our data platform, your WhatsApp number is converted into a hashed value using a one-way cryptographic process, rather than being stored as readable text. Your name and other directly identifying details are not requested or knowingly stored.

9. Data Storage, Security and Retention

Your information is stored on secure data platforms operated by or on behalf of Lexxis. We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, unauthorised access, and misuse.

We distinguish between identifying information and de-identified dataset content, and apply different treatment to each:

  • Identifying information (your WhatsApp number) is not stored in plain, readable form. Instead, it is converted into a hashed value using a one-way cryptographic process before storage, so that it cannot practically be reversed back into your original phone number. This hashed value may be retained for the operative life of the Tongue Trace dataset, so that, for example, we can identify repeat submissions from the same Participant without holding your actual phone number.
  • Voice recordings and text responses forming part of the dataset are retained for the operative life of the Tongue Trace dataset and the AI models trained from it, since the dataset's value and integrity as a training resource depends on it remaining stable and complete over time. This mirrors the approach taken by comparable open African-language speech datasets.

Tongue Trace does not request or collect your location. If this changes in a future version of the Platform, this Policy will be updated accordingly before any such collection begins.

10. Cross-Border Data Transfers

Because Tongue Trace is intended to expand to countries beyond South Africa, and because the Collaborating Parties include international organisations such as the GSMA, your information may be transferred to, stored in, or accessed from countries other than the one in which you are located.

Where this occurs, we will take the steps required by applicable law — including section 72 of POPIA, which restricts the transfer of personal information outside South Africa unless specific safeguards are met — to ensure your information continues to receive an adequate level of protection.

11. Children's Data

Tongue Trace is intended for use by adults.

12. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, including POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Request confirmation of whether we hold personal information about you, and request access to that information;
  • Request that we correct or update inaccurate personal information;
  • Request the deletion of your personal information, subject to our ability to locate your specific records given the de-identification described in Section 8;
  • Object to further processing of your personal information, and withdraw your consent at any time (see Section 13);
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa, or the equivalent regulator in your country, if you believe your information has been processed unlawfully.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 15.

13. Consent and Withdrawal of Consent

Before you are able to interact with Tongue Trace, the chatbot will present you with a summary of this Policy and ask you to expressly confirm that you agree to it — for example, by replying “yes” or a similar clear, affirmative response. You will not be asked to provide any voice recording or text response until you have given this confirmation.

You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us using the details in Section 15. If you withdraw your consent, we will stop collecting further information from you.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time, including as Tongue Trace expands to new countries or languages. Where we make material changes, we will notify Participants who are actively engaging with the Platform and, where required by law, seek renewed consent.
